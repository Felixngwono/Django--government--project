Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I track a government project?

Visit the Project Overview page to browse all projects, or use the search and filter tools to find a specific project by name, agency, status, or priority.

How can I report an issue with a project?

Use the "Report an Issue" link to submit a detailed report with optional evidence. Our team reviews every submission and follows up with the relevant authorities.

Is GovTracker free to use?

Yes! GovTracker is a public transparency platform. All project tracking, budget oversight, and citizen reporting features are completely free for citizens.

How do I submit feedback or a testimonial?

Use the Feedback page to share your thoughts, or the Testimonials page to leave a public testimonial about your experience with a project.

Can contractors apply for tenders?

Yes. Registered contractors can browse open tenders, submit applications with proposals, and track their bid status through the Bids & Proposals section.

How quickly will I get a response?

Our support team responds to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. Urgent issues are escalated immediately to the relevant department.
