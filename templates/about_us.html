About GovTracker

GovTracker is a comprehensive government project monitoring platform designed to bring transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement to public infrastructure and services. We empower citizens, officials, and stakeholders with real-time project tracking, budget oversight, and AI-powered insights — all in one place.

Our Mission

To enhance transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement by providing real-time tracking of government projects. We believe that when citizens can see how public funds are being used — and can participate in the process — governments become more responsive, efficient, and trustworthy.

Explore Projects Contact Us
Our Objectives
  • Monitor public infrastructure projects efficiently and transparently.
  • Ensure projects are completed on time and within budget.
  • Empower citizens to report concerns, submit evidence, and provide feedback.
  • Promote data-driven decision-making for government authorities.
  • Enhance public trust in government project execution.
Platform Features
Everything you need to track, monitor, and engage with government projects

Real-Time Project Tracking

Follow every government project from planning to completion with live status updates, milestones, and progress reports.

Budget Transparency

Track budget allocations, spending, and utilization across all projects. See exactly where public funds are going.

AI-Powered Insights

Leverage AI assistants, risk assessments, and predictive analytics to identify issues before they become problems.

Milestone & Stage Tracking

Monitor project stages and milestones with detailed progress percentages, timelines, and completion status.

Citizen Reporting

Report issues, submit evidence, and provide feedback directly on projects. Your voice drives accountability.

Transparency Reports

Access detailed reports, audit logs, and performance metrics for full oversight and informed decision-making.
